# Agent Policy

This is a public, published research repository.

- Do not create transient notes, session logs, handoff files, scratch
  files, or other private working context. Do not commit them.
- Every result claimed anywhere in this repo must reproduce from the
  scripts in `scripts/` -- no results without a generating script.
- Do not rewrite history on the public remote.
- Keep the repo minimal: paper sources, verifier, evidence scripts, and
  their outputs. Nothing else.
